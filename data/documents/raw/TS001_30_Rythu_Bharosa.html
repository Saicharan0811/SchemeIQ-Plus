Rythu Bharosa

The Government of Telangana launched the Rythu Bharosa Scheme to provide comprehensive support to the agricultural community, ensuring farmers receive timely financial assistance for their needs. This initiative focuses on offering financial aid and access to agricultural resources to empower farmers and improve productivity. The scheme complements other farmer-friendly policies like Crop Loan Waiver and aims to provide working capital to the farmers at the beginning of each crop cycle. By integrating various welfare measures, Rythu Bharosa seeks to enhance the socio-economic conditions of farmers and boost the overall agricultural sector in Telangana, reflecting the government's commitment to rural development and farmer welfare.
