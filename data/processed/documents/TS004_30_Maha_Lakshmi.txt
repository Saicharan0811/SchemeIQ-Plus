Maha Lakshmi Scheme Telangana is a women empowerment scheme that aims to provide:
1. Financial assistance of 2500 Rs for women of Telangana State who are the heads of their families
2. Gas cylinders at 500 Rs, and
3. Free TSRTC bus travel across Telangana.
Beneficiary:
women from BPL card families
